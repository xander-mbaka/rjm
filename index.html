Our farm

RJM Mixed Farm is a family-owned farm of 2.5 acres in Birongo, Kisii County, home to dairy cows, free-range hens and our own hatchery, turkeys, geese, goats, sheep, pigs, rabbits and our farm dogs, plus a herb and spice garden. We run it as a closed loop. Our cows' manure feeds a biogas digester and compost. The compost feeds our vegetables, bananas and fodder. The fodder feeds the cows, goats, sheep and rabbits, and their manure goes back into the compost.

Our hens roam free and eat greens and organic feed, so their eggs have deep yellow yolks. We record every vaccination, spray and harvest, so we can show you exactly how your food was grown.

Our promise

  • Collected fresh: eggs sold within 7 days, milk the same day
  • No synthetic pesticides or fertilisers
  • No milk, eggs or meat sold from animals under medical treatment
  • Anything not fresh? We replace it on the next delivery

🤝 Growing with Birongo

We share compost, banana suckers and Napier splits with neighbouring farmers, host free farmer field days on organic methods, take on student interns, and donate milk and eggs every week to a local feeding programme.
